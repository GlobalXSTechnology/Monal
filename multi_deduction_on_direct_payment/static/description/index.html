Why Withholding Deductions?

Withholding Deduction helps you handle payments that include different types of deductions like income tax, VAT, or service charges. Instead of adding these manually later, the system lets you apply them directly when making a payment. This saves time, avoids mistakes, and keeps your records accurate. It also ensures that the vendor or customer sees the correct payment amount, and your accounts stay clear and up to date.
